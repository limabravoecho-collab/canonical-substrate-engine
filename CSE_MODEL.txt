--------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL DEFINITIONS & COUNTING CONVENTIONS (Axiomatic Base)
--------------------------------------------------
- LIMIT (M): M = 2^31 - 1 = 2,147,483,647.
- POSITION (s): One whole-number location, 0 ≤ s ≤ M. There are 2^31 positions.
- STATE VALUE: Bound energy at s (Nest 1).
- TRANSITION: Movement from one position to the next.
- PHASE (= ARC): One ordered traversal of all positions. Phase 1: 0 to M. Phase 2: M to 0.
- REVERSAL (P): The polarity inversion operator. Reverses the direction of expansion. Not a step, takes no time, does not change the position. Twice per cycle: at 0 and at M.
- STATE VISIT: One occurrence of a position during a cycle. Each counted operation makes one state visit.
- CYCLE COUNT: Exactly 2^32 = 4,294,967,296 counted operations per cycle: 2^31 per phase.
- TRANSITION COUNT: Exactly 2^32 - 2 per cycle. The 2 reversals are not transitions.
- TICK: One advance of the 32-bit runtime counter. The counter is at 0 at the first counted operation of a cycle and advances once for each later counted operation. Its highest value is 2^32 - 1 = 4,294,967,295.
- TIME (Axiom): The ordered sequence of transitions across the macro-cycle. Local time variations: Nest 5, Rules 13 and 14.

NEST 5: Energy Wave Engine (Waves Inside the Sphere of Nest 4)
--------------------------------------------------
RULES:
1. Energy Waves: Waves in the local bound fraction inside the sphere: higher in some places, lower in others.
2. Whole-Sphere Value: The bound fraction of the sphere as a whole (Nest 4, Rule 7).
3. Local Bound Fraction: At any place inside the sphere, at any moment, it can be any value on the Nest 3 spectrum: 0 to 1 (position 0 to position M).
4. Dense Matter Pocket: A place where the local bound fraction is above the whole-sphere value.
5. Void: A place where the local bound fraction is below the whole-sphere value.
6. Location: Dense matter pockets and voids can form anywhere inside the sphere.
7. Conservation: Total energy = 1 at every moment (Nest 1). The excess in all dense matter pockets equals the deficit in all voids.
8. Driver: The shell position s is the only driver of the whole-sphere value.
9. Source of Drift: P is the source of the drift that forms dense matter pockets and voids. It is the only counted operation that is not a transition.
10. Pressure: A place whose local bound fraction is above its surroundings tends to spread toward them: the tendency to even out.
11. Gravity: The pull toward a dense matter pocket. Its strength follows the excess of the local bound fraction over the whole-sphere value. A void has a deficit, so it pulls less than the places around it.
12. Balance: Pressure and gravity work against each other. While they balance, dense matter pockets and voids persist.
13. Local Time: In a dense matter pocket with an extreme bound fraction, local time runs slow. In the places around it, local time runs fast by the same amount.
14. Time Conservation: Over the whole sphere, slow and fast local time cancel. No time is lost.
15. Turn Lag: The shell turns at Pole 1 (Nest 4, Rule 4). Nothing moves more than 1 position in 1 transition (GLOBAL DEFINITIONS, TRANSITION), so the inside of the sphere follows the turn late. The lag at a place is at least its distance from the shell, counted in transitions. That distance ranges from 0, at the shell, to M, at Pole 0. The lag is longer in dense matter pockets (Rule 13).
