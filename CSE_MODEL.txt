- TICK: One advance of the 32-bit runtime counter. The counter is at 0 at the first counted operation of a cycle and advances once for each later counted operation. Its highest value is 2^32 - 1 = 4,294,967,295.
- TIME (Axiom): The ordered sequence of transitions across the macro-cycle. Local time variations: Nest 5, Rules 13 and 14.

13. Local Time: In a dense matter pocket with an extreme bound fraction, local time runs slow. In the places around it, local time runs fast by the same amount.

16. Room for Gravity: The local bound fraction cannot exceed 1 (Rule 3), so the excess (Rule 11) is at most the free fraction, 1 - s / M. The room shrinks as the shell moves outward. One position from Pole 1 it is exactly 1 gap, 1 / M (Nest 3, Rule 2): the smallest possible gravity. At Pole 1 it is 0. This follows from Rules 3 and 11. It is not a new postulate.
