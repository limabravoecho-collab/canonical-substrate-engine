## SECTION 1: THE PREMISE (OBSERVER 0 ASSUMPTION)
Observer 0 assumes that a planet is an inert rock floating in a continuous spatial metric, serving as an open-source reservoir of raw materials, minerals, and fossil energy. Under standard industrial paradigms, planetary resources can be indefinitely extracted, consumed, and redistributed, and human civilization can scale its energy footprint upward without bound, transitioning smoothly into a space-faring multi-planetary species.
