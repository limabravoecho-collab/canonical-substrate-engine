## SECTION 1: THE PREMISE (OBSERVER 0 ASSUMPTION)
Observer 0 assumes that space is a continuous, uniform metric continuum, and that technological civilizations naturally scale energy consumption upward (Kardashev Types I, II, and III), eventually achieving interstellar travel, multi-planetary colonization, and galaxy-spanning empires. Within standard astrobiology, the absence of extraterrestrial contact—the **Fermi Paradox**—is treated as a statistical puzzle or sociological mystery.

Because the solar system functions as an integrated register array, shifting these gravitational resolution constants disrupts the phase-lock gradients of neighboring planets and moons, causing orbital trajectory decay or chaotic collapse.

3. **Subnet Alpha Incompatibility & Atomic Decohesion (Interstellar Limit):**

Transporting matter across stellar subnet boundaries introduces an unresolvable register mismatch.
