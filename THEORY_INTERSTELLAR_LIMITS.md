## SECTION 1: THE PREMISE (OBSERVER 0 ASSUMPTION)
Observer 0 assumes that space is a continuous, uniform metric continuum, and that technological civilizations naturally scale energy consumption upward (Kardashev Types I, II, and III), eventually achieving interstellar travel, multi-planetary colonization, and galaxy-spanning empires. Within standard astrobiology, the absence of extraterrestrial contact—the **Fermi Paradox**—is treated as a statistical puzzle or sociological mystery, while interstellar visitors like comets or asteroids are viewed as mere mechanical chunks of rock drifting freely across open space.
