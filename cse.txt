CANONICAL SUBSTRATE ENGINE (CSE) v1.0: A Deterministic Cosmological Logic Model

SYSTEM: Root Container (Nest 0)
BOUNDARY: All subordinate nested states contained within Nest 0.
TOPOLOGY: Finite Closed Loop (Linearly sequenced by discrete operational steps)

--------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL DEFINITIONS & COUNTING CONVENTIONS (Axiomatic Base)
--------------------------------------------------
- LIMIT ($M$): $M = 2^{31} - 1 = 2,147,483,647$.
- POSITION ($s$): One whole-number location, $0 \le s \le M$. There are $2^{31}$ positions.
- STATE VALUE: The compressed energy at position $s$ (Nest 1).
- TRANSITION: Movement from one position to the next.
- PHASE: One ordered traversal of all positions. Phase 1 goes from $0$ to $M$. Phase 2 goes from $M$ to $0$. A phase is also called an arc (Arc 1, Arc 2).
- REVERSAL ($P$): The polarity inversion operator. It reverses the direction of expansion. It is not a step, it takes no time, and it does not change the position. It happens twice per cycle: at position $0$ and at position $M$.
- STATE VISIT: One occurrence of a position during a cycle. Each counted operation makes one state visit.
- CYCLE COUNT: Exactly $4,294,967,296$ ($2^{32}$) counted operations per cycle: $2^{31}$ in Phase 1 and $2^{31}$ in Phase 2.
- TRANSITION COUNT: Exactly $2^{32} - 2$ transitions per cycle. The 2 reversals are not transitions.
- TIME (Axiom): Time is the ordered sequence of transitions across the macro-cycle. Local time variations are defined in Nest 5, Rules 10 and 11.

- [Kernel-S1] (Descent Vector Initiation): First position of the Phase 2 return traversal (first of 2,147,483,647 decrements).
- Naming: S = Saturation. 0 = the boundary position. 1 = the first position after the boundary.
- Macro-Mapping: This 4-state closed loop, where the 5th entry is the return to [Kernel-0], acts as the immutable template governing macro-cycle execution tiers, distinct from scalar state values.

CYCLE COUNT:

- Phase 1: 2,147,483,647 increments
- P at [Kernel-S0]: +1 (polarity return)
- Phase 2: 2,147,483,647 decrements
- Cycle completes: 4,294,967,296

[Kernel-0] (1) -> [Kernel-1] -> [Kernel-S0] (0) -> [Kernel-S1] -> [Kernel-0] (1)

NEST 2: Time Engine (Two Arcs of Nest 0)
--------------------------------------------------
RULES:
1. Expansion: Expansion = the Nest 0 space count. Minimum expansion = position $0$. Maximum expansion = position $2,147,483,647$.
2. Time Points: Each cycle has 3 time points: 0, 1, 2.
3. Time Units: Each cycle has 2 time units. One time unit = one arc = $2,147,483,647$ transitions.
4. Arc 1 (Time Unit 1): From time point 0 to time point 1. Expansion goes from minimum, at [Kernel-0], to maximum, at [Kernel-S0].
5. Reversal: The reversal $P$ happens twice per cycle: at time point 0, at minimum expansion, and at time point 1, at maximum expansion. $P$ reverses the direction of expansion only. Time does not reverse. $P$ takes no time.
6. Arc 2 (Time Unit 2): From time point 1 to time point 2. Expansion goes from maximum back to minimum, at [Kernel-0].
7. Direction: Time is unidirectional and monotonic inside one cycle: 0, 1, 2.
8. Reset: At [Kernel-0], time resets to 0. Time point 2 of one cycle is time point 0 of the next cycle. The [Kernel-0] flush is this reset. The reset comes before the reversal $P$ at time point 0.

NEST 3: Energy Spectrum Engine (Positions of Nest 1)
--------------------------------------------------
RULES & GEOMETRY:
1. Baseline Origin: Position $0$. Compressed energy = 0. Uncompressed energy = 1.
2. Positions: The spectrum consists of exactly $2^{31}$ positions ($0$ to $2,147,483,647$), ordered by increasing compression, with $(2^{31} - 1)$ gaps between adjacent positions.
3. Terminal Boundary: Position $2,147,483,647$. Compressed energy = 1. Uncompressed energy = 0.
4. Topological Result: The positions form one bounded line, from position $0$ to position $2,147,483,647$.
5. Position Formula: At position $s$, compressed energy = $s / M$, uncompressed energy = $1 - (s / M)$, total = 1, where $M = 2,147,483,647$.
6. Spectrum Content: The positions represent the full spectrum of energy, from electromagnetic radiation (including light) at the least compressed end to matter at the most compressed end.

NEST 4: Sphere Formatting Engine (Shape of Nest 0)
--------------------------------------------------
RULES & GEOMETRY:
1. Pole 0: The centre of the sphere. Position $0$, radius $0$, minimum expansion. Compressed energy = 0. Uncompressed energy = 1.
2. Radial Expansion: Expansion is radial, outward from Pole 0, the same in every direction. Position $s$ = radius $s$.
3. Shell: The shell is the surface of the sphere at the current position $s$.
4. Pole 1: The shell at maximum expansion. Position $M$, radius $M$, where $M = 2,147,483,647$. Compressed energy = 1. Uncompressed energy = 0.
5. Arc 1 (radius grows): The shell moves outward from Pole 0 to Pole 1.
6. Arc 2 (radius shrinks): The shell moves inward from Pole 1 back to Pole 0.
7. Compression: At radius $s$, the compressed energy of the sphere as a whole = $s / M$ (Nest 3, Rule 5). It is the same in Arc 1 and Arc 2, because it depends on the position only.
8. Orientation: The sphere has no axis. North, south and hemispheres do not apply.
9. Topological Result: Space is formatted as a sphere. Compression is bound directly to the radius.

NEST 5: Energy Wave Engine (Waves Inside the Sphere of Nest 4)
--------------------------------------------------
RULES:
1. Energy Waves: Energy waves are waves of compression and rarefaction inside the sphere.
2. Whole-Sphere Value: At shell position $s$, the compression of the sphere as a whole is $s / M$ (Nest 3, Rule 5), where $M = 2,147,483,647$.
3. Local Compression: At any place inside the sphere, at any moment, the local compression can be any value on the Nest 3 spectrum: $0$ to $1$ (position $0$ to position $M$).
4. Dense Matter Pocket: A place where the local compression is above the whole-sphere value.
5. Void: A place where the local compression is below the whole-sphere value.
6. Location: Dense matter pockets and voids can form anywhere inside the sphere.
7. Conservation: Total energy = 1 at every moment (Nest 1). The excess in all dense matter pockets equals the deficit in all voids.
8. Driver: The shell position $s$ is the only driver of the whole-sphere value.
9. Source of Drift: The reversal $P$ is the source of the drift that forms dense matter pockets and voids. $P$ happens at Pole 0 and at Pole 1 (Nest 0). It is the only counted operation that is not a position.
10. Local Time: In a dense matter pocket with extreme compression, local time runs slow. In the places around it, local time runs fast by the same amount.
11. Time Conservation: Over the whole sphere, the slow local time and the fast local time cancel. No time is lost.
